Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms, and Force sends no telemetry. This page covers what is still processed, by whom, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 10 November 2026

Who is responsible

Force and this website are made by Zalán Demeter, who is the controller for the processing described here. Write to hello@useforce.cc with any question or request.

Visiting this site

Loading a page sends your IP address and the usual request details (the address you asked for, the time, your browser's user agent and the referring page). Two services handle these requests:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. delivers the site and protects it from abuse. Cloudflare may also ask your browser to report failed connections to it (Network Error Logging).
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the files on GitHub Pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security.

The purpose is to deliver the site and keep it secure; the legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The logs are kept by Cloudflare and GitHub under their own policies (Cloudflare, GitHub) and are not used to identify or profile visitors.

Fonts, the film and scripts all come from useforce.cc; pages load nothing from other sites and store nothing on your device.

Using Force

Force runs on your own machines and sends nothing about you or your sessions to its developer. It makes these requests:

  • Update check: once a day it reads the install script from useforce.cc to see whether a newer version exists, and force update downloads it from useforce.cc or GitHub. This is the same processing as visiting the site.
  • OpenCode updates: if Force manages OpenCode for you, it asks GitHub for the latest OpenCode release every six hours and downloads it from there.

Claude Code and OpenCode talk to their own providers under those providers' terms; Force does not sit in between. Notifications go only where your own notify command sends them.

Transfers outside the EU

Cloudflare and GitHub are based in the United States. Both are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, restriction of its processing, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. The request logs are held by Cloudflare and GitHub, so write to hello@useforce.cc and you will get help reaching them.

You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or to Hungary's NAIH.
